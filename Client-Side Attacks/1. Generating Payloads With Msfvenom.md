```css
#_Staged payload (two-part)

Consists of a small stager and a larger stage.

The stager is tiny and its job is to create a connection back to your handler and then download & execute the larger Meterpreter stage (or other stage) over that connection.

Example behavior: connect → pull down Meterpreter → execute.

#_Stageless (single-file / “non-staged”) payload

A single self-contained binary that includes both the stager logic and the full payload (stage) in one file.

No follow-up download required — once executed it immediately tries to connect back and run the payload.


```
