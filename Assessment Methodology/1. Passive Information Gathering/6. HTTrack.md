
# 🌐 What is HTTrack?

- **HTTrack Website Copier** is a **free, open-source offline browser tool**.
- It lets you **download (“mirror”) a complete website** from the internet onto your local computer.
- After downloading, you can **browse the site offline** as if you were online.

It’s available on **Windows, Linux, macOS**, and comes in two forms:

- **HTTrack (command-line tool)**
- **WebHTTrack (graphical interface)**
