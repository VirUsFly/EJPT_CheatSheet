###  🌐 Wapalyzer

**Wappalyzer** is a **technology profiler** that detects **software, frameworks, and tools** used by websites.

- It’s available as a **browser extension** (Chrome, Firefox, Edge).
- Also has a **command-line tool** and **API** for automation.
- Widely used in **OSINT, penetration testing, competitive analysis, and SEO research**.
