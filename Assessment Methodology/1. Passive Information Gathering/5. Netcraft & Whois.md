	# 🌐 What is Netcraft?

**Netcraft** is a UK-based internet services company (founded in 1987) that provides **cybersecurity, internet measurement, and anti-phishing solutions**.

It is widely known for its **site profiling tool** (like BuiltWith), but its real strength is in **security intelligence** — tracking **phishing, malware, hosting infrastructure, and certificates** across the internet.
# 🛠 What Netcraft Does (Main Services)
### 1. 🔎 **Site Report / Technology Profiling**

-  **Identifies what technologies a website uses:**
    - **Web server software** (Apache, Nginx, IIS, etc.)
    - **Operating system** (Linux, Windows, BSD, etc.)
    - **Content management system (CMS)** (WordPress, Joomla, Drupal)
    - **SSL/TLS certificate details**
    - **Hosting provider & IP address**

- Example: running a site report on `openai.com` might show →  
    “Cloudflare (server), TLS 1.3 enabled, hosted in the US, first seen in YYYY.”

### 🛠 What WHOIS shows

Depending on the domain and registrar, you might see:

- 👤 **Registrant info** (name, organization, email, phone — unless privacy-protected).
- 🌍 **Registrar** (company where the domain was registered, e.g., GoDaddy, Namecheap).

- 📅 **Important dates**:

- Creation date
- Expiration date

- 🖥 **Name servers** (which control DNS for the domain).
- 🌐 **IP and hosting info** (sometimes).
- 🔐 **Status** (active, locked, pending transfer, etc.).
